03 PAUSE & RESET

Make a little
room to breathe.

You don’t have to earn a break. Try one small reset between the things on your calendar, or whenever your brain feels too full.

A ONE-MINUTE PAUSE ✳
Ready?

Let your breathing stay comfortable and easy. Follow along only if it feels good.

If focusing on your breath feels uncomfortable, stop and try one of the other resets.

⟲
BETWEEN ACTIVITIES · 30 SECONDS

Drop your shoulders

Unclench your jaw. Let your shoulders fall. Shake out your hands, roll your neck gently, and take a sip of water before you switch gears.

◎
WHEN YOUR MIND IS RACING

Come back to right now

Name 5 things you see, 4 things you feel, 3 things you hear, 2 things you smell, and 1 thing you taste. Take your time; you can stop whenever you want.

☼
AFTER THE LAST THING

Make a small landing ritual

Change clothes, put your instrument away, or listen to one song you love. A tiny end-of-day cue can help you shift out of go-mode.

↗
WHEN IT FEELS LIKE TOO MUCH

Tell someone in your corner

Try: “I’m feeling stretched thin. Can we look at this week together?” A trusted adult, coach, teacher, or counselor can help you find support.
